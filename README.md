# Hello explorer! I'm Dekel.

I'm a game designer and C# developer, studying Game Design & Development at Tiltan College of Visual Design and Communication. I turn **ideas into games** — from systems and gameplay design to shipping playable builds.

My background runs from +10 years of hands-on game design in Minecraft server work (economy, systems, and gameplay design) to Unity and C# development today. I like building systems-driven games and I am currently working on becoming a technical artist.

Expanding my knowledge on C# and C++.

## Selected Work

- **TiltanTale** — An Undertale-inspired game set inside Tiltan: fight your teachers in your first year of Game Dev, or spare them for unique endings. Built with MonoGame and ported to the web with KNI — [play it in my portfolio](https://dekeloni2.github.io/portfolio-website/games/play-tiltantale.html).


- **Balatro in C#** — A from-scratch recreation of the roguelike deckbuilder with no engine — the whole game runs inside a console.


- **Brave the Wilderness** — A merge-driven survival game with an economy as its running meta layer, designed to keep the player exploring and fighting.


- **Rest in Profit** — An idle mobile wave management game with a necromancy theme

- **Vampiro** - An incremental survivor game, where you play a cute vampire that rules a castle. Use your servants by consuming them to obtain powerful skills during your runs.


- **Dogfight Evolution** - My case study for hybrid casual mobile games, Dogfight Evolution is a game where you control a plane vertically to shoot down enemy planes. Upgrade your plane and unlock weapons, only to prestige and evolve along with your enemies.
